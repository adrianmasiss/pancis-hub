# Muscle body

Author: DB3D2534 — https://sketchfab.com/DB3D2534
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/muscle-body-48c1ae2debb4491d879ec2020096c407
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adapted for Pancis Hub: merged geometry, normalized scale, reduced from 521,396 to 210,000 triangles (face vertices preserved), preserved source normals, baked short-range ambient occlusion into vertex colors, replaced materials with a procedural matte surface, and added approximate interactive muscle-region highlighting. These regions are a visual guide, not anatomical segmentation supplied by the author.
